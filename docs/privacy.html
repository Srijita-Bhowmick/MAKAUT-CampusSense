Privacy Policy & Academic Terms

Last updated: October 2026 • MAKAUT CampusSense Student Platform

Academic Independence

1. Non-Affiliation & Fair Use Notice

MAKAUT CampusSense is an independent, non-commercial student resource aggregator and study tool developed to help students navigate curriculum modules and review past exam archives. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially associated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT). All course syllabus outlines and past examination questions remain the intellectual domain of the respective academic boards and are referenced here under educational fair-use principles.

Data Privacy & Minimal Collection

2. What Data We Collect & Why

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect the bare minimum information needed to identify your user account:

  • Email Address & Profile Name: Authenticated passwordlessly via Email One-Time Passcode (OTP) and protected by Cloudflare Turnstile to allow you to save personalized study decks and bookmarks.
  • Zero Password Storage: We operate strictly passwordless authentication. We never ask for, hash, or store any account passwords on our servers.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information or study activity to third-party brokers or advertisers.
Session Security

3. Cookies & Session Storage

CampusSense uses strictly necessary functional cookies, local session storage, and Cloudflare Turnstile bot verification to keep your authentication session active and secure against automated attacks. We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies, marketing pixels, or invasive session fingerprinting.

Transparency

4. Academic Inquiries & Content Removal

If you are an academic contributor or institution member with questions, feedback, or concerns regarding any curated resource indexed on this platform, please reach out directly through the Feedback module or contact the platform maintainers via GitHub.
